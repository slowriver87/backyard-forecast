Checking the yard

Ten days of weather, scored for the hours he spends outside watching birds and rabbits.

    How the score works

    Each day gets a score from 0 to 100, shown as the height of the grass. Only daylight hours from 7 AM until 30 minutes before sunset count.

    • Rain, 40%: 100 minus the highest chance of rain.
    • Heat, 35%: the hottest feels-like temperature. 65°F or cooler scores 100. 95°F or hotter scores 0.
    • Humidity, 25%: dew point compared with normal for the date. Normal scores 50. 8°F drier scores 100. 8°F muggier scores 0.

    Soccer balls show the grade. Great (4 balls) is 75 and up. Good (3) is 60 to 74. Fair (2) is 45 to 59. Rough (1) is below 45. The afternoon check uses 3 balls: easy, keep an eye on him, short visits only. The three highest scores are ranked at the top of the page and get gold, silver and bronze medals on their tiles.

    What the numbers mean

    Dew point is the best measure of how sticky the air feels. Higher is muggier. Around 60°F feels fine. 70°F and up feels heavy.

    Feels like is the National Weather Service heat index. Below about 80°F it equals the air temperature.

    Nice hours are daylight hours 80°F or cooler (feels like) with under a 30% chance of rain.

    Afternoon check compares the hottest the afternoon will feel with a typical July afternoon at the same spot. 80°F or cooler is an easy afternoon. 81 to 90°F means keep an eye on him. Above 90°F means short visits only.

    Concession Stand opens when the forecast air temperature reaches 35°F or colder at any hour, day or night. Freezing is 32°F. If the coldest point is in the early morning, it says to put food out the evening before.

    Best stretch of the day is the 4-hour block with the best mix of low rain chance and cooler feels-like temperature.

    How much to trust it

    Days 1 to 7 come from the National Weather Service, the official US forecast. It stops at 7 days, so days 8 to 10 come from Open-Meteo, which serves the NOAA and European computer models.

    Forecasts get less reliable further out. The confidence label (high for days 1 to 3, medium for 4 to 7, low for 8 to 10) is a general rule, not a measured accuracy number.

    The score rules are a rule of thumb picked for his stated likes. They are not a veterinary standard. Use your own judgment on hot days.
